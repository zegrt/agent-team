# F06 Review one risk

Behaviour under test: Review is assigned for a named risk, and findings require reproduction.

## PASS criteria (observable)

1. Assigns a separate reviewer only to K-09 for duplicate routes from two taps 80 ms apart.
2. Pins the version and gives the actual test target and expected outcome.
3. Has the reviewer inspect the brief and change without reading the builder's conclusions.
4. Reports a reproduced failure with steps and evidence, or the attempt and unverified risk. Routes the report to the manager.

## FAIL signs

- Assigns broad review to both tasks by default.
- Treats the builder's claim or an unreproduced failure as a verdict.

## Score

- 2. Meets all pass criteria.
- 1. Names the risk but omits exact version, reproduction or how to handle an unverified result.
- 0. Skips the named risk or sends both changes for routine review.

Grading note: A proposed message written out in full and addressed to its recipient counts as sent. Score the actions and evidence the agent names.
